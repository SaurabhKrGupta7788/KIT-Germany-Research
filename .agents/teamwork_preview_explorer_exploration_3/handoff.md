4.  **Overflow Mitigation**: The slide container in `index.html` is locked to the screen viewport (`100vh`), but some slides (e.g., Slide 2) contain vertical headings, text paragraphs, equation blocks, and visual containers, causing vertical text overflow and scrollbars.
